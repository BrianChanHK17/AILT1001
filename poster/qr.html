Plain-language glossary · for every faculty

Proof — an argument where every step follows from agreed rules; no room for doubt.

Lean — a proof assistant: software that checks each logical step. Not an AI.

Millennium Problem — one of seven US$1M Clay Institute challenge problems.

ETH — 1999 conjecture: some problems provably need exponential time as they grow.

Formalisation — translating a human proof into Lean's checkable language.

P vs NP — if an answer is quick to check, is it always quick to find?
